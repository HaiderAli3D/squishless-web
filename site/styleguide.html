Squishy on the outside, precise on the inside.

Everything the landing page and the app are built from: tokens in tokens.css, components in base.css, the compare slider in compare.js. Switch the theme at the top to check both.

Brand

A photo with a seam running through it: the line the engine removes so nothing gets squashed. Raspberry is the one loud colour; everything else is warm and quiet.

Colour

Use the semantic tokens, never raw hex. Ratios below are measured live against --color-bg (or against the fill the token is meant to sit on) in the current theme.

Type

DynaPuff for headings: puffy, round, a bit of bounce. Figtree for everything you read or click. Sizes are fluid between a 360 px and 1280 px viewport.

Motion

Things move because you touched them. --ease-spring for things that land, --ease-bounce for small pops, --ease-out for everything else. With reduced motion on, durations drop to 1 ms.
